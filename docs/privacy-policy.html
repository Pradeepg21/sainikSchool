Privacy Policy — Sainik School Exam Prep (Sainik Prep)

Effective date: 13 November 2026 · App package: com.satya.sainikschool

Sainik School Exam Prep ("the app") is an educational app that helps students prepare for the All India Sainik Schools Entrance Examination (AISSEE). This policy explains how the app handles information. The app is designed to be used by children together with their parents or teachers.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal information. The app has no account sign-up and no login. It does not contain advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs, and it does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone or files.

2. Information stored only on your device

To make the app work, the following is saved locally on your device and never leaves it:

  • Your selected class (Class 6 or Class 9) and an optional display name
  • Your test results (your last 5 attempts) and your answers, used for review
  • Notes you write in "My Notes"
  • Your study planner timetable

You can delete this data at any time by clearing results inside the app, deleting notes or planner entries, clearing the app's storage in Android Settings, or uninstalling the app. If you have turned on Android device backup, Android may include this data in your own Google backup, under Google's policies.

3. Permissions

  • Notifications: to show the study reminders you schedule in the planner.
  • Alarms & reminders (exact alarms): so your study reminders ring at the exact time you choose.
  • Run at startup (boot completed): to restore your study reminders after the phone restarts.
  • Vibrate: to vibrate for reminders.

None of these permissions is used to collect information.

4. Children's privacy

The app is intended for students aged about 10–15 and their parents. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children or anyone else, and it shows no advertisements.

5. External links

The admission section links to official websites, such as the National Testing Agency and the Sainik Schools Society. These open in your browser and are governed by those websites' own privacy policies.

6. Disclaimer

This is an independent educational app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to the National Testing Agency (NTA), the Sainik Schools Society, the Ministry of Defence or any government body.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
